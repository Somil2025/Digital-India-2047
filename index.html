Transforming India into a Global Digital Powerhouse

By 2047, India envisions a future where every citizen thrives in a digitally empowered ecosystem — driven by innovation, artificial intelligence, and sustainable technology.

About Digital India 2047

Digital India 2047 represents a long-term vision to make India self-reliant and technologically advanced. With a focus on digital infrastructure, skill development, and inclusive growth, the initiative aims to connect every village, empower startups, and drive sustainable innovation.

Vision 2047

By 2047, India’s digital transformation seeks to become a global leader in technology and innovation. The vision focuses on sustainable AI systems, universal 6G connectivity, blockchain-secured governance, and equitable access to opportunities across urban and rural India.

Smart Infrastructure

  • Development of interconnected Smart Cities
  • Green data centers powered by renewable energy
  • AI-driven traffic, waste, and power management systems
  • Public access to digital resources in rural areas
  • Next-generation broadband and satellite networks
